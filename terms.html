Article 1Who we are

TROUF is the trade name of Nouhaila Matrouf, a sole trader (eenmanszaak) at Nassaustraat 15, 2000 Antwerp, Belgium. Company and VAT number BE 1040.969.950. Email: Nouhaila@troufagency.com. In these terms she is called “TROUF”, and the party asking for a quote or giving an assignment is called “the client”.

Article 2Scope

  1. These terms apply to all quotes, assignments and agreements of TROUF, including talent management, business development, influencer marketing, brand partnerships, creative concepts, social strategy and events.
  2. By accepting a quote or giving an assignment, the client accepts these terms. The client’s own terms do not apply unless TROUF accepts them in writing.
  3. Deviations only apply if agreed in writing; an email is enough. If the quote or contract says something different from these terms, the quote or contract prevails.

Article 3Quotes and agreement

  1. A quote is valid for 30 days unless it states another period.
  2. The agreement is formed as soon as the client accepts the quote in writing (email included), or as soon as TROUF starts the work at the client’s request.
  3. A quote is based on the information provided by the client. If that turns out to be incomplete or incorrect, TROUF may adjust price and planning in consultation.

Article 4Performance

  1. TROUF carries out every assignment carefully and to the best of her ability. This is an obligation of means: TROUF does not guarantee any specific result, such as reach, views, followers, sales or attendance.
  2. The client provides all information, materials, feedback and approvals on time. Delays on the client’s side may shift the planning and lead to extra costs.
  3. TROUF may work with third parties such as talents, photographers, venues and suppliers. TROUF selects them carefully but is not liable for their shortcomings, unless caused by her own fault.
  4. Deadlines are indicative unless expressly agreed as binding.

Article 5Working with talent

  1. In brand partnerships TROUF acts as the talent’s manager and intermediary, unless agreed otherwise. The specific arrangements (deliverables, planning, feedback rounds, usage rights, exclusivity and fee) are set out in the quote or collaboration agreement.
  2. The talent creates the content in their own style. Unless agreed otherwise, the brand gets one feedback round on the concept and one on the content.
  3. The brand only uses the content within the agreed channels, period and scope. Wider use, such as paid ads, whitelisting or use outside social media, requires prior approval and an extra fee.
  4. Exclusivity only applies if expressly agreed, with a clear product category and duration.
  5. Commercial content is always clearly identified as advertising, in line with the applicable rules and recommendations for influencer marketing. The client will not ask for anything that goes against them.
  6. New paid collaborations with talent the client met through TROUF run through TROUF during the assignment and for 12 months after it.

Article 6Prices and payment

  1. Prices are in euro and exclude VAT. Costs not included in the quote, such as travel, production or venue costs, are charged separately after the client’s approval.
  2. Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date unless stated otherwise. TROUF may ask for a deposit, especially for events and productions.
  3. In case of late payment, interest is due by operation of law and without notice of default under the Belgian Act of 2 August 2002 on combating late payment in commercial transactions, plus a fixed compensation of 40 euro for recovery costs. TROUF keeps the right to reasonable compensation for higher recovery costs.
  4. If an invoice remains unpaid, TROUF may suspend further work after written notice.
  5. Comments on an invoice must be made in writing within 8 days of receipt.

Article 7Changes and cancellation

  1. Changes after acceptance are made in consultation and may affect planning and price.
  2. If the client cancels an assignment in whole or in part, the client pays for the work already delivered, the costs and commitments to third parties that can no longer be cancelled (such as talent fees, venue and suppliers), and a compensation of 30% of the remaining amount of the assignment.
  3. For events, the quote may set out specific cancellation periods. Those then prevail.

Article 8Intellectual property

  1. Concepts, ideas, strategies, presentations and other material by TROUF remain the property of TROUF. After full payment the client receives a right to use them for the agreed purpose. Any other use or passing them on to third parties requires written consent.
  2. Proposals that do not lead to an assignment remain TROUF’s. The client will not carry them out, directly or through third parties.
  3. TROUF may show a completed assignment (name, logo and visuals) in her portfolio, presentations and social media, unless confidentiality was agreed or the client asks in writing not to.
  4. The client guarantees that material it provides, such as logos, images, texts and music, does not infringe third-party rights, and indemnifies TROUF against related claims.

Article 9Confidentiality

Both parties keep the other party’s confidential information secret, such as prices, contracts, plans and figures. This continues after the assignment ends.

Article 10Liability

  1. TROUF is only liable for damage caused by her own proven fault.
  2. Liability is limited to the amount invoiced for the assignment concerned, excluding VAT.
  3. TROUF is not liable for indirect damage, such as loss of profit, missed opportunities or reputational damage, nor for decisions of platforms (such as algorithms, blocks or outages) or the conduct of third parties.
  4. Complaints about the work must be reported in writing within 8 days of delivery or of the event.
  5. These limitations do not apply in case of intent, fraud or gross negligence.

Article 11Force majeure

If a party cannot meet its obligations because of circumstances beyond its control, such as a talent’s illness, extreme weather, government measures or platform outages, those obligations are suspended. If the force majeure lasts longer than 30 days, either party may end the assignment without compensation. Work already delivered is paid for.

Article 12Personal data

TROUF processes personal data in line with the privacy statement below.

Article 13Governing law and disputes

  1. Every agreement is governed by Belgian law.
  2. In case of a dispute, the parties first try to find a solution together. If that fails, the courts of Antwerp, Antwerp division, have jurisdiction.
  3. If the client is a consumer, the mandatory rules of consumer law continue to apply in full.

Article 14Final provisions

  1. If a provision is invalid, the other provisions remain in force. The invalid provision is replaced by a valid one that comes as close as possible to its intent.
  2. TROUF may change these terms. For an ongoing assignment, the version that applied at acceptance remains in force.
  3. These terms are a translation of the Dutch “Algemene voorwaarden”. In case of differences, the Dutch version prevails.

When you email TROUF or work together, TROUF processes your name, contact details, company details, the content of your messages and, where needed, billing details.

  • To prepare quotes and carry out assignments, based on the agreement.

  • For talents: the data needed to manage them and arrange collaborations, based on the agreement.

Article 4Who data is shared with

  1. Only with those who need it: service providers such as email, invoicing and accounting software and the accountant, and brands or talents to the extent needed for a collaboration. Your data is never sold.
  2. Some providers, such as GitHub and Google, may process data outside the European Union. They do so with the safeguards required by privacy law.

Article 5How long data is kept

No longer than needed. Invoices and accounting records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires. Contact emails that lead nowhere are deleted at the latest 2 years after the last contact.

Article 6Your rights

  1. You can access, correct or delete your data, restrict processing, object, and ask for your data to be transferred. Just send an email.
  2. If you are not satisfied, you can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels, dataprotectionauthority.be.

This privacy statement may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
